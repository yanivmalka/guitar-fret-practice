How to read a medal

Two things at a glance: tier and instrument

A struck medal tells you two independent things. The metal is the tier you’ve reached. The emblem stamped into it is the instrument. They never trade places, so a Gold always looks Gold and a guitar medal is never mistaken for its bass twin.

Two instruments, two sets

Guitar and bass, struck side by side

A guitar family and its bass counterpart ask for the same thing on different necks, so they are kept as two separate medals. Under the new tiers the difference is only in the emblem — the metal for a given tier is the same on both.

String Master. Guitar draws four brisk string waves; bass draws one thick, slack one. Both here are Silver — the tier reads the same, the emblem says which neck.

Full String Master. Six string lines on guitar, four on bass. The Gold metal is shared; only the count in the emblem changes.

Neck Runner. A short board on guitar, a longer 24-fret board on bass. Both Bronze here — same strike, different neck.

Milestones are shared. Century, Marathoner, the streaks, accuracy and speed families are one set of medals, earned for life, fed by guitar and bass history at once — there is no separate bass copy.

Role medal

Admin Role Granted, not earned · no tiers

Marks the people who keep the app running. An admin reads every idea posted to the Feedback board, not just their own, and moderates what comes in.

  • Onyx granted Your account is flagged as an app administrator. It appears on its own, always struck, and can’t be practised toward. It isn’t counted in a player’s family total.

How the book keeps score

  • Session families are checked the moment a round ends. Lifetime families are recomputed from your entire history every time this book opens, so old practice retroactively lifts them.
  • Reaching a rung is permanent. Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum are each stamped once, with the date you got there, and the celebration for a rung fires a single time.
  • Fretboard-shape families — String Master, Full String Master, Neck Runner, Both Ends, Low End — are kept separately for each instrument. Every milestone family is one shared medal, evaluated over guitar and bass history together.
  • Every medal you already held before the tiers arrived is now that family’s Bronze — nothing was lost, and the higher rungs are simply waiting.
  • Medals survive Clear History and Clear all history. They’re a record of what you did, not a running stat.
  • They’re still awarded in Silent mode and with the score hidden — only the end-of-round celebration needs the score on screen.
